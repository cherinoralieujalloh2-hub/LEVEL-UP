Today's lesson · Discipline

Discipline means keeping a promise to yourself, even when you don't feel motivated.

Don't wait until you feel ready. Start with a small action you can repeat.

Example: Instead of saying, “I'll read for two hours someday,” read two pages today. Repeating small actions builds a habit.

Try this (10 minutes): Write down one goal that matters to you. Break it into one action you can do today, then complete it.

Reflect: What small promise can I keep to myself today?
